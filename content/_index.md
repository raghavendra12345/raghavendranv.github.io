I am a research scholar at the Department of Computer Science and Automation,
Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, working under
[Dr. Arpita Patra](https://www.csa.iisc.ac.in/~arpita/) in the CrIS Lab.

I completed my Master's at IIT Madras, where I worked with
[Dr. Shweta Agrawal](https://www.cse.iitm.ac.in/~shwetaag/) in the Department
of Computer Science and Engineering. My M.S. thesis was titled *Multi-key
Homomorphic Encryption and Secret Sharing*. Before that I read Computer Science
for my undergraduate degree at NIT Goa.

I am interested in learning about new cryptographic primitives and applying
them to real-world problems. Away from the desk I play cricket, table tennis
and badminton, and spend time on music and photography.
