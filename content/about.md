I work on the broad area of Cryptography and focusing on Multi-Party Computation (MPC).
This site is where I write up the things I had to work out slowly.

Most posts assume comfort with undergraduate algebra and probability. Where a
proof matters I write it out rather than gesture at it. Where I am unsure, I say so.
