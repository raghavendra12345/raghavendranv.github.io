## About

I work on cryptography at the CrIS Lab, Department of Computer Science and
Automation, IISc Bengaluru. Most of what I write here is the long version of
something I had to work out slowly — the notes I wish had existed when I was
first reading the material.

Most posts assume comfort with undergraduate algebra and probability. Where a
proof matters I write it out rather than gesture at it, and where I am unsure,
I say so. Corrections are always welcome.
